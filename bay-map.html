Aurora Sailing · Bay Winds. Automatic wind observations from the Bureau of Meteorology, plus the clubs’ own stations at Sandringham, Brighton, Mornington and Blairgowrie (live readings, no 24-hour history). The Bureau stations refresh about every 10 minutes, so "Updated N min ago" cycles up to roughly 10–15 minutes then drops — that range is normal. Automatic data isn’t quality-checked and can carry errors — always confirm conditions on the water before you sail.
