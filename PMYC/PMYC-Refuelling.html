Refuelling — Power Boats

Fuel storage · mixing · filling

← Back to the pre-session check

All three boats run two-stroke fuel. Mix it, fill on the ramp, and put everything back in the flammables cabinet.

Storage

  • Jerry cans live in the flammables cabinet when not on a boat. Close it after use.
  • Large cans = unmixed 91 unleaded (yellow + black). Both marked UNLEADED.
  • The green tank is the two-stroke mixing tank.
  • Boat tanks and fuel lines store in the tool shed over winter — full tanks in the flammables cabinet.

Mix — two-stroke 50:1

50:1 — 20 mL of oil per 1 L of petrol
Option 1 — in the green tank: 5 L of 91 unleaded + 100 mL two-stroke oil → shake → pour into the boat tank.
Option 2 — in the boat tank: add oil at 20 mL per litre. Check the tank’s size first.

Fill

On the ramp by the shed only — never on board, on the beach or on sand.
  • Use a funnel. Don’t overfill. Mind your back with the big cans.
  • Return the cans to the cabinet and close / lock up the shed (the roller door needs a programmed fob).

Never / Always

  • Never mix unleaded and mixed fuel.
  • Never put unleaded in a mixed-fuel can (without mixing), or mixed fuel in an unleaded can.
  • Never refuel a tank while it is on the boat.
  • Always label every tank and can with the fuel it holds.
