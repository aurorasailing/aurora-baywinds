Start Sailing 1–3 at a glance

What each course covers, grouped by syllabus element. A paraphrased planning reference for session prep — for the exact wording and the instructor sign-off boxes, use the official Australian Sailing Dinghy Program Syllabus & Log. Each course is a nominal 12 hours.

Start Sailing 1 first skills, assisted

Preparation

  • Right clothing for the conditions
  • Identify and use the safety gear
  • Why protection from the elements matters

Boats & equipment

  • Rig the boat with help
  • Name the commonly used parts of the boat

Ropework

  • Tie a figure-eight knot

Safety & emergencies

  • Capsize recovery in shallow water
  • Take part in single-boat towing

Skills & techniques

  • Launch and recover with help
  • Tack and stop the boat
  • Sail a windward / broad-reach course
  • Paddle around a marked course

Sailing theory

  • Port and starboard
  • Windward and leeward
  • Overtaking vessels

Weather & tides

  • Identify wind direction
  • Find a weather forecast

Start Sailing 2 sailing unassisted

Preparation

  • Right clothing for the conditions
  • Identify and use the safety gear
  • A basic pre-sailing warm-up

Boats & equipment

  • Rig the boat unassisted
  • Name all parts of the boat
  • Bowline, clove hitch, round turn & two half hitches
  • Care for the boat, gear and sails

Safety & emergencies

  • Capsize recovery in deep water
  • Take part in multiple-boat towing

Skills & techniques

  • Launch and recover unassisted
  • Crew, helm & set sails on all points of sail
  • Gybe correctly
  • Sail a triangle & windward / leeward course
  • Man-overboard recovery
  • Tiller extension through all manoeuvres
  • Correct centreboard for each point of sail
  • Heave to

Sailing theory

  • Apply the basic sailing rules
  • Rules — port/starboard, w'ward/leeward, overtaking, collisions, commercial, power v sail
  • Explain the points of sail

Weather & environment

  • Wind direction & strength
  • Respond to gusts and lulls
  • Find a forecast · aware of local tides

Better Sailing stronger winds, more speed

Preparation

  • Right clothing for the conditions
  • Identify and use the safety gear
  • Warm-up and stretches before sailing

Boats & equipment

  • Rig the boat to suit the conditions
  • Name all parts of the boat & sails
  • Care for the boat, gear and sails
  • Rig spinnaker & trapeze (where fitted)

Safety & emergencies

  • Recover a fully inverted capsize
  • Take part in multiple-boat towing

Skills & techniques

  • Launch & recover in any wind direction
  • Roll tack, roll gybe, sail backwards
  • Set the sails to advantage
  • Sail a trapezoidal & slalom course
  • Come alongside a vessel / pontoon
  • Hold a position at a mark
  • Spinnaker & trapeze (optional)

Sailing theory

  • Controls that adjust sail shape
  • Adjust sail shape to conditions
  • Apply the rules in a fleet
  • Identify local buoyage

Weather & environment

  • Spot wind shifts
  • Respond to gusts and lulls
  • Read tidal flow on the water
