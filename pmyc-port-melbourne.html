What the pictures mean

  • 🚣Too calm 0–5 kt — not much wind, grab the oars.
  • ⛵️Sailing weather! 5–15 kt — a great breeze to sail in.
  • 🛟Getting strong 15–23 kt — be careful out there.
  • 🌪️Too windy 23–30 kt — best to wait.
  • ☠️Stay ashore 30+ kt — too wild to sail.

Where’s this from?

This is the wind from Fawkner Beacon — the Bureau of Meteorology’s marine beacon out on the bay, the nearest station to Port Melbourne.

We keep the wind direction simple — one of eight main points: North, North-East, East, South-East, South, South-West, West and North-West.

The time shows when Fawkner Beacon last reported — it updates about every ten minutes, so the count climbs to around ten or fifteen minutes then starts again.

Wind from the Bureau of Meteorology at Fawkner Beacon. Always check conditions on the water before you sail.
