Aurora Sailing · Bay Winds. Automatic wind observations from the Bureau of Meteorology, plus the clubs’ own stations at Sandringham, Brighton, Mornington and Blairgowrie (live readings, no 24-hour history). Reading the numbers:
  • Refresh — the Bureau refreshes these stations about every 10 minutes, so "Updated N min ago" cycles up to roughly 10–15 minutes and then drops. Any value in that range is normal.
  • Gust — the strongest gust in the station's latest reading (the Bureau's logged peak, not just the steady wind).
  • Trend — whether the wind has been building or easing over the last couple of hours.
  • F5 fresh breeze — the Beaufort force and its name, a 0–12 scale for wind strength.
  • Club panels — the four club stations show a live reading each, with no gust or trend.
Observations are automatic and not quality-checked — always confirm conditions on the water before you sail.
