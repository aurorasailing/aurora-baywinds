OAS Boating — Stages 1–3 at a glance

What to cover each stage, grouped Plan · Do · Review. A paraphrased planning reference for session prep — for the exact statements and the verifier sign-off columns, use the official Scouts Australia OAS Boating sheets.

Stage 1 first time afloat

Plan

  • Dressing for conditions — layers, sun protection, closed shoes
  • The buddy system — what it is
  • PFDs: what they are and why you wear one
  • Staying with your buddy and the boat in a capsize
  • Looking after your gear for the trip
  • Tying a reef knot

Do

  • Swim 10 m wearing a PFD
  • Risks of weather exposure
  • Getting in and out of the craft safely
  • A short boating trip — at least 1 hour
  • Where bow and stern are

Review

  • Talk about what you enjoyed

Stage 2 building the basics

Plan

  • Choosing the right-size PFD
  • Fitting and adjusting a PFD
  • Pre-launch safety precautions — weather, tides, where & who with
  • Why the right clothing and safety gear matter
  • Four actions to take if you capsize
  • How to signal for help
  • Labelling the essential boating equipment

Do

  • Swim 25 m wearing a PFD
  • Protecting yourself from sun and elements
  • Rigging with help; getting in and out safely
  • Two trips — about 1.5 hrs each
  • Where to sit and your role aboard
  • Cleaning the boat down before storage

Review

  • Reflect on two trips — enjoyed / learnt / improved
  • What you'd do differently next time

Stage 3 toward independence

Plan

  • DRSABCD first-aid steps
  • Weather-condition risks — people, gear, environment
  • Your environmental impact when boating
  • Personal items to carry — by trip and conditions
  • Finding and reading the local forecast
  • State / Branch water-safety rules
  • Six naturally-powered watercraft
  • Helping a junior fit their PFD
  • Three more boating knots
  • Six parts of a boat and/or oar
  • The difference between a tack and a gybe

Do

  • Five trips logged by end of Stage 3
  • Self-rescue back aboard with help
  • HELP and huddle positions in a PFD
  • Swim 50 m in a PFD — 25 m, tread 2 min, 25 m
  • Teaching the buddy system to a Stage 1

Review

  • Discuss boat-safety learnings from two trips with your Patrol
Plan = know it · Do = show it on the water · Review = talk it through. Swim and trip counts are cumulative toward each stage. Planning reference · paraphrased from the Scouts Australia OAS Boating stages 1st Vic Sea Scouts · built with Aurora Sailing
